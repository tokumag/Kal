**Source Guidelines:**

## Danger Sign Rules Matrix

| Rule ID | Clinical Danger Sign | Target Stage | Urgency Verdict | Clinical Evidence Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `RULE_PREECLAMPSIA` | Severe headache, visual disturbance, facial swelling | PREGNANT | `SEEK_CARE_NOW` | WHO IMPAC MCPC Sec 2; FMOH Guideline #1 |
| `RULE_BLEEDING` | Vaginal bleeding | PREGNANT / POSTPARTUM | `SEEK_CARE_NOW` | WHO PCPNC Guide Sec B2; FMOH Obstetric Hemorrhage Protocol |
| `RULE_FEVER` | High fever (>38°C), chills | ALL | `SEEK_CARE_NOW` | WHO Sepsis Protocol; FMOH Infection Control Guide |
| `RULE_ABDOMINAL_PAIN` | Severe abdominal pain / uterine tenderness | PREGNANT | `SEEK_CARE_NOW` | WHO IMPAC Sec 3 Abdominal Assessment |
| `RULE_FETAL_MOVEMENT` | Decreased / absent fetal movement | PREGNANT (>28w) | `SEEK_CARE_NOW` | FMOH Antenatal Care Fetal Wellbeing Protocol |
| `RULE_NEWBORN_DANGER` | Jaundice, lethargy, refusal to feed | NEWBORN | `SEEK_CARE_NOW` | WHO IMCI Young Infant Guidelines |
| `RULE_MILD_SYMPTOMS` | Mild nausea, mild ankle swelling without headache | PREGNANT | `MONITOR` | FMOH Community Health Extension Worker Manual |
